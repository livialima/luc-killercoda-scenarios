### Check disk space

Type: `df -h`{{exec}}

Shows how much [disk space](https://www.man7.org/linux/man-pages/man1/df.1.html) is used and available.

---

### Check folder sizes

Type: `du -h`{{exec}}

Displays the [size](https://www.man7.org/linux/man-pages/man1/du.1.html) of files and directories.
