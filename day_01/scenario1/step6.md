### Display your username

Type: `whoami`{{exec}}

This prints the [user name](https://www.man7.org/linux/man-pages/man1/whoami.1.html) you logged in with.

---

### See who is logged in

Run: `who -a`{{exec}}

Displays [who is logged on](https://www.man7.org/linux/man-pages/man1/who.1.html) into the server (and their terminal sessions).

---

### See what users are doing

Type: `w`{{exec}}

Shows logged-in users and [what they are doing](https://www.man7.org/linux/man-pages/man1/w.1.html).
